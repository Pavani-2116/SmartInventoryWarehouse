# 5–8 Minute Evaluation Video Script

1. Login as Admin (30s)
2. Show dashboard KPIs, charts and low-stock alerts (60s)
3. Open Inventory, filters and SKU simulation (45s)
4. Add a product and refresh to demonstrate SQLite persistence (45s)
5. Perform Stock IN and show updated dashboard/movement history (45s)
6. Attempt invalid Stock OUT above available quantity and show rejection (45s)
7. Create a purchase order, receive it, and show inventory update (60s)
8. Show warehouse and supplier pages (45s)
9. Show Reports + CSV export (30s)
10. Mention architecture, RBAC, SQLite transactions and deployment (30–60s)
